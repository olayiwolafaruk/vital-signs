Pregnant? Some fish contain more mercury than others

WhatsApp FacebookX
Add to Google

Fish can be nutritious during pregnancy. Choosing the species matters.

Mercury can affect a baby’s developing brain and nervous system. Large predatory fish such as swordfish tend to contain more mercury than smaller species.

What to do
Avoid swordfish during pregnancy. Choose lower-mercury options such as sardines or tilapia rather than cutting out fish altogether.

Ask the seller for the species rather than relying on the shape of a cut steak. If you cannot identify a fish, choose a familiar lower-mercury option such as sardines or tilapia. The FDA’s fish chart groups species into choices to eat more often, less often or avoid. Use local advisories if eating fish caught from waters with contamination warnings.
