Can mouldy groundnuts harm your liver?

WhatsApp FacebookX
Add to Google

Some moulds produce aflatoxins. You cannot judge a whole batch by taste.

Aflatoxins can contaminate groundnuts. Long-term exposure increases the risk of liver cancer. A normal-looking nut is not proof that a batch is toxin-free.

What to do
Buy from reliable sellers, store nuts dry and discard mouldy or damaged nuts. Don’t rely on roasting to rescue spoiled food.

Visible mould, discolouration or damaged nuts are reasons to reject food, not a complete detection system. Toxins may be present without an obvious warning sign. Smelling the bag or tasting a few kernels cannot certify the rest. Groundnut paste can also be made from contaminated nuts; grinding them changes the texture, not their history.
