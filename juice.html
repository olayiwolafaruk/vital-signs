What does sipping juice all afternoon do to your teeth?

WhatsApp FacebookX
Add to Google

How often your teeth meet sugar matters, even with unsweetened fruit juice.

Bacteria in dental plaque use sugars to produce acids that wear down enamel. Juice contains free sugars even when no sugar is added.

What to do
Choose whole fruit more often. If you drink juice, have it with a meal rather than sipping between meals.

Imagine two people drinking the same small glass of juice. One finishes it with lunch. The other keeps it on a desk and takes a mouthful every few minutes through the afternoon. The second pattern gives plaque bacteria repeated supplies of sugar. Each time they use it, the acids they produce can draw minerals out of the tooth surface.
